# Source

Where a value came from, beside it: read off a record or a document, in a solid tag; worked out by the agent, in a dashed one, to check; changed by the person, in the accent.

- Solid: what was read, and from where: a receipt, a card, an email, a record, a profile, the calendar.
- Dashed: what was inferred. "Dashed tags are inferred: check them."
- Accent: what the person changed. Their change stays theirs; the original is kept.
- Any line opens to its source, and can be changed.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app.
