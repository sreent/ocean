# SignOff

What will happen, exactly, before it does: each line, changes included, who decides next, who prepared it, what sending it does, and the one thing to do. The person signs off on it, and sends it.

- Each line as it will be sent, with the person's changes marked.
- Who decides next, by name, and the evidence.
- Who prepared it, and what that agent may not do: "assistant, which can't approve or pay".
- What sending it does, in the colour of what it does: here, a change to a supplier's record, in `--changes`.
- The one thing to do, worded for this outcome ("Ask for the change", "Submit claim"), a way to make a change, and that it's signed and kept in the audit log.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app. Here it carries a change to a supplier's bank details.
