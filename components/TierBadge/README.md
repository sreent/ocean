# TierBadge

Where something runs: someone's own space, a team's, or production. Proposed.

- Told by line, not colour, so it never competes with what a tool does or how something stands: dashed for someone's own (as everything not yet in force is), solid for a team's, and production in ink, twice as heavy.
- Each product keeps its own word inside it: a7c's tiers are personal, team and production; a3t's are a personal space's Sandbox, a team's Preview and Staging, and Prod.
- Put it beside a run, a deployment, a promotion or a case: wherever it matters where something happened.

**Takes:** `tier` (`personal`, `team`, `production`) and the product's word.

The preview is a static rendition of the proposed piece.
