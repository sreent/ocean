# FlowPatterns

What a flow can do, drawn: a condition on a step, a step its condition skipped, a wait, a step that failed and what it stopped, a run's path, a change held for a decision, and a call the checks refused. A showcase card, proposed.

- A condition is a label on the curve into the step it guards, in the product's words ("when days < 5").
- What a run didn't use fades, a step its condition skipped among it, and says why ("skipped: days was 12").
- A wait is a step of its own, and neutral: it does nothing but wait.
- A failed step has the cross in its corner and says what failed. The steps it stopped fade.
- A run's path is green: the curves it took, and what it used of what hangs from the agent.
- A change held for a decision has the clock in its corner.
- A call the checks refused is `--bad`: the step, its curve, and its words.
