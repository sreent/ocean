# ChangeRequest

A change request in a3t, in Ocean: where a change to a team's app is decided before it reaches Prod. A showcase page, proposed.

- The frame is a3t's: its pages under the four headings, the change request's own page beside them.
- Its head says what it is, where it comes from (a working copy in Ana Ortiz's space) and where it goes (from release 1.3.2 to 1.4.0), and how it stands, as a badge with its shape.
- The path from the working copy to Prod, with the one thing to do now.
- What it changes, in words. Who gains or loses access, in words, each line in the colour of what the access does: seeing reads, changing writes, exporting crosses the organisation's edge. That last widens who may take data out, so a security admin reviews it, as a3t's guardrails ask.
- What it does to the data, in words: what's added, and that nothing is dropped.
- On the side, its checks and the decision, with Approve and Request changes. The author can't approve their own change.

The preview is a static rendition, at a desktop's size.
