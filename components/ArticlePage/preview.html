Database failover

BACK-A-4
Changed by Anna Schmidt Mon 9 NovVersion 4

When the primary fails, promote the replica before anything else: the pooler keeps queueing for 30 seconds, and the app retries after that. The last drill was BACK-48.

Promote the replica

  1. Check it's caught up: SELECT pg_last_wal_replay_lsn();
  2. Promote it with pg_ctl promote.
  3. Repoint the pooler, then the app.

Never promote a replica that's more than a minute behind without asking the lead.
