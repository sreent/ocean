# FlowDesigner

a3t's flows on the workbench: here, a flow that waits for people, live. A showcase page, proposed.

- The frame is a3t's, as in AppDesigner: the rail with its mark, its pages, and on the corner of Tasks how many wait; and a toolbar that says which app this is, that this is a working copy of its release 1.3.2 in a personal space, whether it's saved, and its problems. Its views are the parts of an app (Data, Forms, Pages, Flows and Permissions), and its one thing to do is to ask for the change.
- The canvas is the whole middle. Which flow, and Design or Live, sit at its top left; adding a step at its top right; zooming at the left of its foot; Try it in the middle, which runs the flow on a record in the working copy; and Canvas or List at the right.
- Live, each step that waits says how many cases wait there now, and the longest wait.
- The inspector holds the chosen step, in a3t's words: who does a person's task, its outcomes and what each runs, its reminders and its deadline, what the person sees, and the cases that wait there now. The flow's own panel says how it starts: by hand, by an action, by itself on a record, or on a schedule, with when it runs next. A step that stays with a person says so.
- The steps to add open from the left, as Picker draws them, grouped as LogicKinds groups them.

The preview is a static rendition, at a desktop's size.
