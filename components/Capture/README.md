# Capture

The camera, for what the outcome needs from paper: a receipt, an invoice, an ID, a meter. The one step that needs the person's hands.

- It frames the document and takes the photo by itself once it's in the frame, so it's one tap; the person can still take it, or choose a photo or a file.
- It says what it found as it finds it ("Receipt found. Hold still…").
- Its view is its own dark (`--viewfinder`), and the document keeps its paper (`--paper`), in either theme.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app.
