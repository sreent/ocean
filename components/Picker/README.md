# Picker

The parts someone adds to a canvas, at the workbench's left: what each product's definitions allow, grouped, each with its icon in the colour of what it does. A showcase card, proposed.

- a7c's: its six kinds of step, what starts a run, then its connectors by name, each logo small and in one ink.
- a5s's: the scenario's tools by what they do, a5s's own abilities, and what starts a run.
- Each part is a button. It adds the part after the chosen step, or someone drags it onto the canvas.
- Finding a part searches names and what they do.
