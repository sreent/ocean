# AppDesigner

a3t's app designer, in Ocean: where someone builds an app's form in their own space, on the workbench. A showcase page, proposed.

- The frame is the workbench's: a rail of a3t's pages, and a toolbar that says which app this is, that this is a working copy of its release 1.3.2 in a personal space, whether it's saved, and its problems. Its views are the parts of an app (Data, Forms, Views, Pages, Process, Permissions), and its one thing to do is to ask for the change.
- On the left, the fields to add, by kind: basic, records, files and layout.
- In the middle, the form as the people who use the app will see it, on its sheet. The chosen field wears the focus ring, and a dashed line in `--accent` shows where a dragged field would land.
- A field tagged PII is masked for every role that may not see it, and says so.
- On the right, the chosen field's settings, in a3t's terms: its label and id, the record it looks up, whether it's required, when it shows (an expression of CEL), who may see it and change it, role by role, and its classification.
- The change request turns those permissions into words for its reviewers.

The preview is a static rendition, at a desktop's size.
