# Canvases

The family's canvases side by side: a7c's skill and a5s's agent, as n8n lays a canvas out, and a3t's flow, as Mendix does. Each product draws its steps the way its builders know. All three share Ocean's grammar, so they read as one family. A showcase card, proposed.

- The same colours for what a step does: a step that reads, a change to a record in coral, what crosses the edge in violet, and an agent as the lagoon square.
- The same states in a step's corner (a tick, a clock, a cross, a count of problems), the same green path where a run went, the same fading for what it didn't use, and the same focus ring.
- The same canvas, the same words on arrows, and the product's name and mark.
- What differs is how steps are drawn and joined: squares with their names beneath, joined at dots by curves (a7c and a5s), or boxes with their words inside, joined by arrows at right angles (a3t).
