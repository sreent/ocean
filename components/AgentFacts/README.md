# AgentFacts

An agent's facts, as food carries a label: the same for every agent, from its release alone.

- First, what it may do: whom it works for, its autonomy, what it reads, writes and changes, any outside content, and the data it handles.
- Then a part such as how it does per run: model calls, tokens, cost, graded samples. Say which figure it is ("median") and over when.
- At the foot, who owns it.
- Heavy rules in `--text` set its parts apart, as a food label's do. It's 24rem wide at most, and leads the side of an agent's page.
- In a7c, the same label fits a skill: its declared capabilities, its tiers and its autonomy at each.

**Takes:** `name`, `version`, `facts`, `more`, `foot`.

**In a5s:** `studio/src/design/agentic.tsx`. The preview is a static rendition.
