# OnItsOwn

The line no rung crosses, drawn. At the ladder's top rung, Acts, the agent moves a meeting on its own and says so at once. It never pays on its own, at any rung. A showcase card.

- On its own: anything its rung and limits allow that doesn't vouch for something in the person's name and can be taken back. It comes as a Status at once, with what it did, why, what allowed it, Undo, and "Ask me first next time".
- Never on its own: what vouches for something, such as submitting a claim, and what can't be taken back, such as a payment. The agent prepares those, and the person signs them off.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md).
