# Question

What only the person can answer: what the agent couldn't decide, or what breaks a rule. It waits on them, in `--warn`, with its clock.

- Its title names the line it's about, and its figure.
- One sentence says why it's asked and what the answer sets: "It sets the expense type, and who approves it."
- The answers are buttons, the likely ones first; or a short field, when it needs words, such as a reason for an approver.
- Once answered, it folds to a line with a tick, and what the person chose.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app. Here it asks for three capabilities.
