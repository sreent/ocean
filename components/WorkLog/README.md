# WorkLog

The agent's work while it happens: each step ticked when it's done, where each piece came from, the one under way, and a promise of what isn't done yet.

- Each step is a line: what the agent did, and under it where it found it (the calendar, a card, an email, a record).
- The step under way turns; nothing else moves.
- It ends with the promise that matters to this outcome, beside the shield of the product's own checks: "Nothing is sent until you confirm it."
- Brief: it shows the work, not a log. The details are a tap away, on the run's timeline.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app. Here it puts together a leave request.
