# Status

How an outcome stands, brought to the person: where it is, who it waits on, what comes next, and how long it can still be taken back. Or, when the agent acted on its own, what it did, at once.

- Its stages, in order: done with a tick, the one now with a clock and who it waits on, the rest to come.
- Until when the person can take it back (recall, withdraw), and the button to.
- The agent says it will message the person when it moves, so nobody has to check.
- When the agent acted on its own, at the ladder's top rung: what it did, from what to what, in the colour of what it did; why, and what allowed it; Undo, and until when; and "Ask me first next time", so a person can always take back autonomy for themselves.
- Never for what vouches for something in the person's name, or can't be taken back: those come as a SignOff, at any rung.

In a4a, what the assistant did on its own sits in the conversation at once, beside its run, as the agent's card (`.talk-body > .draft.acted`): 28rem wide at most, flat, its facts read from the start, and "Ask me first next time" a link at the start of its own line.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app. Here it tracks an access request, which ends by itself, and says what the agent did on its own: it moved a meeting.
