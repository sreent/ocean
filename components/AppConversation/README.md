# AppConversation

An app made with a3t, used in conversation at a desktop's size: the conversation kit's pieces (docs/conversation.md), in the app's own frame. A showcase page, proposed, with sample data.

- The app carries its own name and pages (Expenses: Ask, My claims, To approve), on Ocean underneath. Its agent answers under the app's name, and is marked as an agent by its mark.
- On a wide screen, the draft pins beside the conversation and stays the live one: it changes as Ben answers, instead of being posted again. On a phone it sits in the talk (ExpenseClaim).
- The conversation keeps the work: what Ben asked, his photo of the receipt, and what the agent did, with where it found each piece.
- The draft puts the two questions first. With room to show it, what was filled in is open, each value with its source.
- It opens as a page too. Every outcome an a3t app prepares is also a record in the app, with its form, for anyone who'd rather fill it in.
- The composer takes the next thing: a correction, a question, or another outcome.

The preview is a static rendition, at a desktop's size.
