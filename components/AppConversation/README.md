# AppConversation

An app made with a3t, used in conversation: someone says what they want done, and the app answers with an outcome card. A showcase page, proposed (docs/platforms.md, the runtime, in conversation).

- The app carries its own name and mark (Expenses), on Ocean underneath; its pages are its own (Ask, My claims, To approve).
- Ben says what he wants, in his own words. The app answers with the outcome as it understood it: the claim, for which project, how much, from how many receipts.
- Then what it will do, step by step, each in the colour of what it does: it reads his receipts, writes a draft only he sees, and sends the claim to his manager, which changes a record; Finance pays it, not the app.
- Then only what it still needs from him, already filled where it could be, each line traced to the receipt it came from.
- Then who decides, and what the app may do: draft and send, never approve or pay.
- One thing to do: Send it to Priya. Or change it, or open the same claim as a page.
- The composer below takes the next thing he wants, with a few suggestions.

The preview is a static rendition, at a desktop's size.
