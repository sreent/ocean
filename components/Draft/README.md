# Draft

What the agent prepared, before anything happens: what it is, how it stands, its key figure, the questions only the person can answer, first, and the rest, folded, with where each value came from.

- Its head says what the outcome is, that it's a draft and whose (only theirs until they send it: a write, not a change), and its key figure: a total, a balance, a date.
- Exceptions first: the questions that need the person sit at the top, counted, waiting on them. Everything the agent could fill is folded under "Filled in for you".
- Its one thing to do waits until the questions are answered, and says so.

It belongs to the conversation kit (docs/conversation.md), for any capability, not one kind of app. Here it carries an expense claim; the Capabilities page shows it carrying others.
