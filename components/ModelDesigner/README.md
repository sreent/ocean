# ModelDesigner

a3t's data model on the workbench: each entity a box of its fields, with arrows for its references and its lines, as Mendix's domain model draws them. A showcase page, proposed.

- On the left, what someone can add: an entity, a reference, lines, and each kind of field, in the tags the form designer uses.
- On the canvas, each entity's fields in order, with a short note where it helps (how many choices, worked out, a file). A reference, and a record's lines, are arrows to the entity they name, with a word on each. An entity new in this working copy says so.
- On the right, the chosen field's settings: its label, whether it's required, its classification, and who may see it, role by role.
- The change request turns access into words for its reviewers.

The preview is a static rendition, at a desktop's size.
