# FlowList

A canvas as its list: the same steps, in order, for the keyboard and for a phone. Every canvas has one, and a narrow screen starts with it. A showcase card, proposed.

- The choice of the two is a choice of views (`.modes`): Canvas and List, at the right of the canvas's foot, and at the top of the list.
- The list keeps the canvas's order: what starts a run, then each step in order; or what starts an agent's runs, the agent, what it works with, and what each run ends in.
- Each row is a button, with its icon in the colour of what it does, its name, what it does, its condition, and how it stood or its problems at its end.
- Choosing a row opens its settings in place, as the inspector shows them beside the canvas. The keyboard reaches each row in order: Enter opens one, and Escape closes it.
