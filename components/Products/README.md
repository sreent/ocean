# Products

The same frame for a5s, a7c, a3t and a4a: each product's name and mark, and its pages under the same four headings.

- a5s's pages are its own, from `shell.js`. a7c's, a3t's and a4a's are proposed, from their own design documents: a7c works in cases, findings and runs, builds skills and connections, and decides on approvals and policies; a3t works in tasks and apps, builds in someone's own space, and decides on change requests and deployments; a4a works in issues, boards and plans, builds projects, and oversees time and cost and the people on them. A heading with no page, such as a4a's Decide, isn't shown.
- Nothing else sets a product apart: not a colour, not a typeface, not a shape. Someone who uses all four finds every page where its heading says it is.

This is a showcase page, not a piece to draw. See `docs/products.md` for how each product's words map onto Ocean.
